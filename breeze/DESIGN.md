## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an airy, modern, high-clarity visual aesthetic tailored for an immersive visual discovery product. Built around white space, vibrant visual media, and energetic signature accents, the interface recedes to let visual wallpaper content command primary focus while maintaining effortless touch targets and navigational clarity.

### Personality & Tone
- **Airy & Effortless:** Generous spacing, soft contrast, and clean neutral backdrops make the gallery experience feel weightless.
- **Expressive & Bold:** Punchy typography scale combined with an electric violet signature (#6B4DF7) delivers a contemporary consumer feel.
- **Tactile & Fluid:** Soft contours, full-pill controls, and floating actionable surfaces invite tactile discovery.

### Design Movement
A modern synthesis of **Clean Consumer Minimalism** and **Tactile Edge Modernism**: pure neutral light backdrops, dynamic image containers, soft inner rims, and pill-shaped interactive anchors.

## Colors

The palette establishes high content clarity through bright surfaces paired with deliberate chromatic accents.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#6B4DF7`):** The signature electric violet used for dominant actions, primary focus states, and key identity touches.
- **On-Primary (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast optical white for text and icons atop primary assets.
- **Primary Soft (`#F0F1FF`):** Tonal violet tint applied to chips, active pills, badges, and selected item backplates.
- **Secondary (`#E07D47`):** Warm terracotta accent used for curated editorial badges, hot trends, and thematic collections.
- **Tertiary (`#4EAC6D`):** Fresh spring green reserved for live wallpaper indicators, download confirmations, and positive utility states.

### Neutrals & Surfaces
- **Background & Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas and primary container level.
- **Surface 2 (`#F3F5F7`):** Recessed wells, image skeleton loaders, segmented pickers, and passive card backdrops.
- **Line (`#E2E4E7`):** Subtle boundaries, hairline rules, and structured grid outlines.
- **Ink (`#181A1C`):** Maximum contrast editorial headings and primary readable body copy.
- **Ink 2 (`#66686D`):** Balanced secondary body text, metadata, creator attributions, and timestamps.
- **Inverse (`#1C1E20`):** Dark contrast anchors for toasts, bottom floating toolbars, and contextual tooltips.

### Status Indicators
- **Success (`#159A49`):** Download complete, synced, or active subscription tokens.
- **Warning (`#C97D00`):** Storage consumption warnings, battery usage prompts on dynamic wallpapers.
- **Danger (`#D32F34`):** Destructive actions, cache purge, and download failure signals.

## Typography

Typography relies on a geometric, friendly, and legible neo-grotesque structure. Headings command visual hierarchy with confident, tight tracking and heavy weights, while body and metadata roles maintain generous line heights for instant readability against image-dense displays.

### Hierarchy & Application
- **Headline XL / Headline LG:** Hero titles, curated daily feeds, collection headlines, and onboarding statements.
- **Headline MD / SM:** Category titles, collection card headers, and sheet titles.
- **Body LG / MD:** Creator bios, wallpaper details, descriptions, and settings descriptions.
- **Body SM:** Secondary metadata (aspect ratios, file sizes, resolutions, color hex tags).
- **Label Tokens:** Used across pill buttons, chips, filter tabs, and numeric badge counts.

### Iconography Linkage
Icons pair with typography using `Material Symbols Outlined`, set with a default optical weight of 400 and grade 0, matching the cap-height of respective text labels.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a vertical-scrolling, fluid multi-column rhythm engineered to display photographic aspect ratios (9:16, 9:19.5, and 1:1) seamlessly across viewport widths.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 600px):** 2-column staggered masonry or rigid 2-column grid. Outer horizontal canvas margin is `margin-sm` (16px), column gutter is `gutter-sm` (12px).
- **Tablet (600px - 1024px):** 3-column to 4-column dynamic grid. Outer canvas margin is `margin` (20px), column gutter is `gutter` (16px).
- **Desktop / Large Screens (> 1024px):** 5-column or 6-column gallery layout constrained to a maximum centered container of 1440px. Outer canvas margin expands to `margin-lg` (40px) with `gutter-lg` (24px).

### Spacing Principles
- Component inner padding standardizes on `space-md` (16px) for cards and inputs.
- Micro component spacing (icon-to-text, label-to-badge) enforces `space-xs` (8px).
- Section stacks enforce `space-xl` (32px) to maintain breathable boundaries between feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages crisp surfaces, transparent boundary lines, and diffused ambient illumination. Hard drop shadows are avoided to maintain an open, modern atmosphere.

### Depth Scales
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Pure `#FFFFFF` canvas. Groupings are established using `#F3F5F7` fills or 1px `#E2E4E7` borders without shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Ambient Layer):** Media cards and list rows. Layering is achieved via a subtle border `1px solid rgba(226, 228, 231, 0.7)` combined with an ambient blur:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(24, 26, 28, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 26, 28, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action & Overlays):** Interactive floating pill controls, circular 40px action buttons, and active filter bars:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(107, 77, 247, 0.18), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(24, 26, 28, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Sheets):** Full preview drawer sheets and image context menus:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(24, 26, 28, 0.12)`.

### Glass & Blur Elements
Sticky navigation headers and floating action bars use a semi-translucent backdrop filter (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) to keep user context grounded in continuous wallpaper imagery.

## Shapes

The interface embraces soft geometry with distinct visual hierarchies between containers and direct touchpoints.

### Corner Radii Guidelines
- **Standard Cards & Image Tiles:** 12px to 16px corner radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`). Wallpaper preview tiles utilize 16px corners to mimic actual modern device screen silhouettes.
- **Secondary Containers & Sheet Surfaces:** 16px to 24px corner radius on top sheets and bottom dialogs.
- **Interactive Buttons & Chips:** 9999px (full pill-shape) for all horizontal action buttons, search bars, and selectable chips.
- **Circular Icon Controls:** Dedicated 50% border radius for 40px circular functional icons (favorite, download, share, back).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Full-pill (`rounded-full`), height 48px, background `#6B4DF7`, text `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`), no border. Pressed state scales down to 98% with `#583BD9`.
- **Primary Soft Pill:** Background `#F0F1FF`, text `#6B4DF7`, no border. Used for secondary inline actions (e.g., "Preview", "Apply Filter").
- **Circular Icon Button:** Dimensions 40px × 40px, fully circular (`border-radius: 50%`), background `#FFFFFF` with Level 2 elevation or `#F3F5F7` flat. Material Symbol icon centered at 20px optical size.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Selection Chips:** Height 36px, horizontal padding 16px, pill-shaped.
  - *Inactive:* Surface `#F3F5F7`, text `#66686D` (`label-md`), no outline.
  - *Active:* Surface `#6B4DF7`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle glow `0 4px 12px rgba(107, 77, 247, 0.25)`.
- **Counter / Badge Chip:** Height 24px, pill-shaped, background `#F0F1FF`, text `#6B4DF7` (`label-sm`).

### Wallpaper Cards
- **Preview Photo Tile:** High-ratio container (9:16 aspect ratio), corner radius 16px. Background defaults to `#F3F5F7` placeholder skeleton while loading.
- **Overlay Action:** Bottom gradient vignette (`rgba(24, 26, 28, 0.4)` to transparent) with circular 40px like/download icons positioned at the bottom-right using `space-sm` offsets.
- **Pro / Dynamic Badges:** Placed at top-left corner, 10px radius, blurred translucent backdrop (`rgba(24, 26, 28, 0.6)`), text `#FFFFFF` (`label-sm`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Global Search:** Height 48px, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background `#F3F5F7`, border `1px solid transparent`.
- **Text & Focus State:** Text `#181A1C`, placeholder `#66686D`. On focus: background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #6B4DF7`, ambient focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(107, 77, 247, 0.12)`.
- **Leading & Trailing Controls:** Material Symbols Outlined (Search `#66686D` left, Clear/Mic right).

### Lists & Collection Rows
- **List Items:** Padding 12px vertical, 16px horizontal. Separator line 1px solid `#E2E4E7` inset by 56px to align with text start.
- **Collection Strip:** Horizontal snap-scrolling row, gap `space-md`, cards featuring 12px corner radius with thumbnail and stacked typography (`headline-sm` over `body-sm`).

### Checkboxes, Toggles & Radio Buttons
- **Toggles:** Track width 48px, height 28px, pill shape. Active track `#6B4DF7`, inactive track `#E2E4E7`. Thumb is `#FFFFFF` circular (22px diameter) with soft drop shadow.
- **Radio / Checkbox:** 20px box/circle, `border: 2px solid #E2E4E7`. Selected state switches border and fill to `#6B4DF7` with white check or dot interior.
